10 decisions
  • Purchase target: OpenSea's per-token page, built from the contract and token ID
  • Purchase appears only once a piece has been opened, since it acts on that piece
  • Selector order: A–E, matching the token IDs
  • Each page's 2KB engine-state jsonUri stays on Irys (the pages embed their engine block, so it isn't fetched)
  • No stop-on-minimize, matching 3xGP and R3MIX
  • No pointer-events-none on the iframe while dragging: a title-bar drag across the piece already works
  • No extra focus handling: the first click on an inactive OBH window brings it forward, later clicks reach the piece
  • About gets its own cream background over the window's black, like 3xGP's About
  • Purchase opens with noopener
  • The window was built before the Blob upload; only the vendored pages changed afterwards
